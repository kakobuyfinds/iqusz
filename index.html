From listing to parcel

Confirm the seller and variant

Open the CAFANS listing and choose KakoBuy under Buy Link where available. Match the seller, color and size on the agent’s page to your shortlist. Save the selected variant, its current price and the date you checked it.

Measure first, then review QC

For shoes, check whether the seller’s chart measures the foot or the insole. For apparel, compare flat chest width, length and sleeve length to a garment you own. Use warehouse quality-control photos, when available, to check size tags, stitching and visible damage before approving shipment.

Compare the total parcel cost

Start with the variant price, then add shipping to the warehouse and any agent charges. For international quotes, compare the same destination and packed dimensions across eligible lines. Record currency, quote date and any import charges separately so you can see what each quote includes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-link catalog for shoppers using the KakoBuy agent. This independent guide lists a selection of CAFANS shoes and clothing with reference item prices and links to the original CAFANS listings.

Are the catalog photos warehouse QC photos?

The cards show listing photos. Review the warehouse QC photos for your specific order when the agent makes them available.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The CNY amount is the reference price for the item. Your selected variant, agent charges and shipping quote determine your payable total.

How do I save a shortlist?

Select products and choose Download selected CSV. Open the file in a spreadsheet app to record your variant, sizing, QC, shipping quote and the date you rechecked the listing.
